Slide 3: What You Built

Dual-Stream Architecture & BUILD 2026 Deliverables

End-to-end multi-disease screening pipeline integrating calibrated ensembles with 6-qubit quantum simulation.

System Execution Flow (Inputs → Processing → Result)
1. Clinical Intake & Sentinel Scan: Accepts vitals (Age, SBP, DBP, Glucose, Lipids, Creatinine). Sentinel scanner rejects physiological zeros (e.g. Glucose=0) to prevent corrupted inputs.
2. Dual-Stream Feature Preprocessing: Stream A scales data for classical models (StandardScaler). Stream B maps features to \([0, \pi]\) for Angle RY Hilbert Space embedding on a 6-qubit register.
3. Parallel Ensemble & VQC Inference: 5 Classical Models (RF, SVM, LR, HGB, XGB) execute alongside PennyLane 6-Qubit Parameterized VQC with Ring CNOT entanglement in <2ms.
4. Platt Calibration & Consensus Fusion: Applies sigmoid scaling fit on unaugmented validation splits. Blends outputs via cross-validation optimal weights.
5. Decision & Abstention Output: Calibrated Risk Score (%), Agreement Level, Feature Sensitivity Breakdown, and automated Abstention if spread \(|\Delta P| > 0.85\).

Slide 5: Ingenuity and Technical Decisions

Why Hybrid Consensus & Platt Calibration?

Engineering clinical reliability through calibrated consensus fusion and explicit algorithmic abstention.

01 • Originality: The Abstention Contract

Knowing When the Model Doesn't Know

Most clinical ML models hallucinate a high-confidence answer on garbage inputs. ArogyaDristi enforces an explicit Abstention Guardrail: when classical models and quantum VQC diverge by >0.85, or inputs violate biological constraints, the system explicitly refuses to guess, prompting clinical re-examination.

02 • How It Differs from Existing Tools

Dual Euclidean & Hilbert Space Geometry

Standard tools use static heuristics (Framingham) or isolated neural nets. ArogyaDristi projects data simultaneously into classical Euclidean feature space and a 64-dimensional quantum state space (\(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes 6}\)), leveraging cross-paradigm consensus to catch borderline cases that single architectures miss.

03 • Key Architectural Decision

Angle RY Encoding over Amplitude Encoding

Amplitude encoding requires deep state preparation circuits (\(\mathcal{O}(2^n)\)) unsuited for NISQ simulation. We selected Angle RY Rotation (\(\theta_i = \pi \cdot x_i\)): it keeps circuit depth constant \(\mathcal{O}(1)\), maps directly to individual biomarkers, and maintains physical interpretability on the Bloch sphere.

04 • Challenge Faced & Overcome

Raw Probability Divergence Resolved via Platt Scaling

Tree models (XGBoost/RF) generate raw uncalibrated probabilities that diverged from quantum expectation values, triggering false abstentions. We solved this by implementing Platt scaling fit strictly on an unaugmented validation split, harmonizing cross-paradigm probabilities without data leakage.
